# Urban Company Service-Ops Dashboard Stories & Stakeholder Narratives

This document contains the official stakeholder narratives derived from the reconciled Urban Company Service-Ops diagnostic dataset (`city_category_summary.csv`)[cite: 1]. Each narrative follows the required **Headline → Evidence → Implication** structure, tailored for operational leadership[cite: 1].

---

## 1. City Ops Lead Narrative: SLA Breach Rate & Regional Risk Analysis

* **Headline:** Pune exhibits the highest total revenue volume but requires immediate operational intervention due to concentrated SLA breaches, whereas Delhi NCR maintains tighter delivery compliance.
* **Evidence:** Across the 600 total bookings in the reconciled dataset, the overall SLA breach rate stands at **13.2%** (79 total breaches)[cite: 1]. Examining city-level performance from the dashboard[cite: 1], **Pune** leads total revenue at **₹2,28,727**[cite: 1] across its category distribution, but records a disproportionate cluster of service delays. Conversely, **Delhi NCR** (total revenue of **₹1,40,771**)[cite: 1] and **Mumbai** (**₹1,51,430**)[cite: 1] maintain lower incident densities relative to their booking volumes.
* **Implication:** City Ops leads must reallocate field support and partner onboarding oversight toward high-volume hubs like Pune and Bengaluru (**₹1,79,835**)[cite: 1] to curb partner congestion and eliminate avoidable SLA penalties, protecting customer trust in rapid-growth markets.

## 2. Category Lead Narrative: Revenue Concentration & Under-Booked Service Lines

* **Headline:** Deep Home Cleaning dominates aggregate revenue generation, while Salon for Men and Plumbing remain significantly under-booked relative to their pricing capacity.
* **Evidence:** Total marketplace revenue across all categories reaches **₹10,47,973** from **600 bookings**[cite: 1]. **Deep Home Cleaning** is by far the strongest revenue driver, single-handedly generating **₹50,8964** (nearly 49% of total company revenue) due to high ticket sizes[cite: 1]. In contrast, **Salon for Men** produces only **₹73,563** in revenue[cite: 1], and **Plumbing** records **₹80,837**[cite: 1], reflecting lower transaction frequencies and smaller basket sizes across urban centers.
* **Implication:** Category leads should design targeted bundling campaigns—such as combining grooming or minor plumbing services with high-value deep cleaning packages—to lift utilization rates in lower-performing categories and balance marketplace dependency.
